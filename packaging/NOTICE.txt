Cine CMR QC 0.2.3

This package is intended for research and annotation quality-control workflows.
It is not a medical device and does not provide a clinical diagnosis.

ED/ES estimates are initialized from label-180 cavity-area extrema and require
doctor confirmation. Patient ejection fraction is entered manually; the plugin
does not infer clinical EF from independent single-slice series.

Before deployment, validate image/mask orientation, label semantics, write
permissions, backup creation, and JSON audit output on local test data.
